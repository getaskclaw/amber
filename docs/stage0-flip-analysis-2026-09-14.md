## 6. What this does and does not show

- **Same-day determinism is real.** luna-high r1 vs r2: byte-identical fail set; glm-5-2's defect reproduced twice; every observed flip is cross-day or cross-week. Day boundary is where the wobble lives.
- **Version drift is not instability.** deepseek preview→GA flips (`14/23→16/23`) track a silent model swap — arm identity must pin model version before any rerun counts as a repeat.
- **No rates yet.** Deepest repeat is n=3. `0/2` and `0/3` runs prove nothing about recovery probability; they prove the measurement exists and the events are real.
- **Prose evidence is flagged.** Rows with `evidence=prose` (luna r3, medium re-sweep, infra makeups, anchor reruns) are documented in results files but have no published per-case matrix — they cannot be re-verified cell by cell from public artifacts.
